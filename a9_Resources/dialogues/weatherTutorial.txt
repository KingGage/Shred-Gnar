Hey there, shredder! Jonny back at it again. Before we keep tearing up this mountain, there’s somethin’ you’ve gotta understand if you want to ski like you mean it.

Not every day rides the same. Some days are dreamy and smooth. Some are deep and floaty. And some… well, some will knock your socks off.

But the magic trick? Knowing which skis work best for the conditions you’re about to hit.
Dial this in, and you’ll feel like the mountain’s finally speaking your language.

Ahhh, the legendary groomer day—sun shining, skies glowing, perfect corduroy under your boots. This is the kind of snow begs for some carving.

On hardpack like this, you want skis that are precise, fast, and grippy. Your best choices:
Race Skis: Narrow, stiff, and laser-focused. Perfect edge hold on firm snow.

Carving Skis: Deep sidecut, responsive, and built for smooth, controlled turns.

Mogul Skis (if you're bump hunting): Narrow and quick edge-to-edge.

Your All-Mountain skis will do just fine too—but the narrow, stiff options? Yeah… they’ll make groomer days feel like a dream.

Fresh snow overnight? Congrats, my friend—you’ve stumbled into a pow day, and trust me, these are the days you’ll remember for years.

Powder changes everything. You’re not riding the ground anymore—you’re floating on top of it. To stay up, not sink, you want skis designed for maximum float.

Your MVPs here:
Powder Skis: Wide underfoot, lots of rocker, made to surf the deep stuff.

Big Mountain / Freeride Skis: Stiff, powerful, stable in deeper snow and variable terrain.

Try taking skinny skis out here and you’ll discover what it’s like to be a human snowplow. Spoiler: it’s not great.

Not every day is a postcard. Some days the sky goes gray, visibility is flat, the snow turns icy or choppy, or the mountain gets scraped down to the firm stuff.

When conditions get unpredictable, you want skis that can smooth out the bumps and stay stable no matter what’s under you.

Your best tools:
All-Mountain Skis: Balanced flex + rocker make them confidence boosters when snow is weird.

Big Mountain Skis: Handle speed and trash snow like champs.

Touring Skis: Rockered tips help in windblown, crusty, or mixed conditions.

Bring something too narrow or too stiff into messy conditions? You’ll feel every bump like it’s a personal insult.

Before every run, the Ski Shop will show you the upcoming weather conditions. That means you’ll always know what you’re gearing up for—pow, groomers, ice, crud, or anything in between.

Choose your skis wisely, trust the conditions board, and make the most of whatever the mountain throws your way. I’ll see you in the shop—be ready to gear up and drop in like a pro.
