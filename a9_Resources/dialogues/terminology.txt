That was a sick run! You may have noticed that your turns felt a little slower. Your skis are all-mountain which work great for almost everything.

But they aren’t your best options on hard pack groomers like what you just rode. You'll learn how ski specs change how the ski performs.

First you need to know some basic terminology. Three things you gotta understand to speak ski: underfoot width, rocker, and stiffness.

First up is underfoot, or waist width. That's the measurement right under your boot, the skinniest part of the ski. We're talking millimeters here.

Now rocker, look at a ski from the side. See how the tip and tail curve up off the snow? That's rocker.

Last one is flex or stiffness. This is how much muscle it takes to bend the ski.

These three things shape almost everything about how a ski feels and performs on the snow. Get the combo right, and you'll be set.
